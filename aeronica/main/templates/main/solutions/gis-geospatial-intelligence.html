Capabilities

What We Deliver

A comprehensive set of capabilities powered by indigenous hardware and AI-driven analytics.

Web GIS Dashboards

Interactive web-based GIS platforms with layer management, measurement tools, time-series comparison, and role-based access for stakeholder coordination.

Digital Twin Creation

High-fidelity 3D digital twins of cities, industrial plants, and infrastructure assets. Integrated with IoT data streams for real-time monitoring and simulation.

Spatial ML & AI Analytics

Custom machine learning models for land-use classification, change detection, object recognition, and feature extraction from multisource geospatial data.

GIS Data Integration

Seamless integration with existing enterprise GIS (ESRI, QGIS, AutoDesk). We convert, clean, and schema-map your spatial data into unified geodatabases.

Vegetation & Environmental Monitoring

NDVI/NDRE time-series analysis for crop health, forest cover change detection, and environmental impact assessment using multispectral satellite and drone data fusion.

Urban & Regional Planning

Land-use mapping, zoning analysis, infrastructure density assessment, and population distribution modelling for urban development authorities and smart city missions.

Methodology

How We Deliver Results

A structured, repeatable process refined across hundreds of missions.

01

Spatial Requirements Analysis

We define your data schema, output formats, accuracy standards, and integration requirements with existing enterprise systems. A spatial data model is designed for your specific use case.

02

Data Acquisition & Fusion

Drone imagery, satellite data, LiDAR point clouds, and IoT sensor data are fused into a unified spatial data fabric. All sources are georeferenced to a common coordinate system.

03

Analytics & Modelling

Aeroni.AI runs spatial ML models, change detection algorithms, and statistical analyses. Results are validated against ground truth data and calibrated for accuracy.

04

Deployment & Handover

Delivered as a web GIS platform, API-integrated digital twin, or standard geospatial file package. Training and documentation included for your team.
