Delivery Model

From Brief to Intelligence

A structured, repeatable process that ensures every mission delivers exactly what you need—on time, in spec, and fully compliant.

01

Consult & Plan

We analyse your objectives, site conditions, regulatory requirements, and data output needs. A detailed mission plan is created with flight paths, sensor configuration, and timelines.

02

Deploy & Capture

DGCA-certified pilots deploy with the optimal platform for your mission. RTK GPS, multispectral, LiDAR, or thermal sensors capture data at maximum resolution.

03

Process & Analyse

Aeroni.AI processes raw data through SfM photogrammetry, AI inference, and spatial analysis. Orthomosaics, point clouds, NDVI maps, and defect reports are generated.

04

Deliver & Support

You receive structured deliverables&mdash;geotiff, SHP, PDF, or live dashboard. Our team provides a walkthrough and remains available for follow-up analysis.
