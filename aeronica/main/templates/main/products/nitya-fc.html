Nitya FC

Flight Controller & Avionics

India's Most Advanced Indigenous Flight Controller

The Nitya FC is Aeronica's fully indigenous flight controller — built from 4 years of dedicated embedded systems R&D by our in-house team. Powered by the STM32H7 microcontroller running at 480 MHz with FPU, it delivers triple-redundant IMU sensing, military-grade 256-bit AES encrypted telemetry, and a custom PX4 firmware stack purpose-built for Aeronica's agriculture and survey platforms.

Three independent sensor units work together to ensure accuracy. If one fails, the others keep flying. That's the redundancy critical systems need.

Military-Grade Security

Dedicated encryption chip protects all data. Your operations stay secure, whether commercial or government.
