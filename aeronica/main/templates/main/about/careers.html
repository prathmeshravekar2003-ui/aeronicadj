Careers at Aeronica

Work That Matters

At Aeronica, you're not building software for someone else's hardware — you own the entire stack. Our engineers design airframes, write flight firmware, build AI models, and process field data from real missions. If you want to work on technology that has a measurable impact on Indian agriculture, infrastructure, and national security, this is where you belong.

Our Culture

Build from First Principles

We design everything in-house. If it doesn't exist, we build it. Our team has the freedom and responsibility to engineer solutions from scratch.

Outcome Oriented

Every project has a real-world mission — a farmer's crop, a railway corridor, a mining site. We measure success by client outcomes, not internal metrics.

Small Team, Big Impact

50+ people. 12 Indian states covered. We operate like a startup but with the credibility of a DGCA-certified product company.

Continuous Learning

We sponsor external training, conference attendance, and academic collaboration. Your growth is our growth.
