Why Aeronica

Not Just Another Drone Company

India's drone market is flooded with assembled products from imported components, rebranded Chinese hardware, and generic solutions with no after-sales support. Aeronica was founded to be different — and after 5 years, our client results speak for themselves.

What Sets Us Apart

100% Indigenous. Zero Import Dependency.

Our drones are designed and manufactured in India, This means no export control restrictions, no supply chain vulnerabilities, and full IP ownership. Critical for government and defence clients.

DIPP Recognized | All IP India-Owned | Manufactured in Pune

The Only DGCA Type-Certified Agricultural UAV

The Samrudhhi-10L holds DGCA Type Certification for agricultural operations. This independent verification of airworthiness gives clients confidence in reliable, compliant commercial deployment.

DGCA Type Certificate Holder | First in Category

Full-Stack Technology Ownership

We own and maintain the entire technology stack — hardware, firmware, GCS software, and data processing AI. This means faster customisation, faster bug fixes, and zero dependency on third-party vendors for critical system components.

Nitya FC | Aeroni.AI | Custom GCS | Composite Airframes

Proven at India's Largest Scale

450+ km of railway corridor surveyed for Central Railway. 15,000+ flight hours logged. 50+ enterprise projects across 12 Indian states. Our solutions are proven at the scale India's infrastructure demands — not lab-tested prototypes.

Central Railway | JSW Steel | IOCL | BASF | Govt of Maharashtra

Purpose-Built for Indian Conditions

Indian summers, monsoon humidity, high-altitude Himalayan terrain, and dense urban interference are not afterthoughts in our design process — they're the design parameters. Our platforms are field-tested in the conditions your operations actually face.

Field-Tested: 45°C+ Heat | Monsoon Humidity | High-Altitude

End-to-End Partner, Not Just a Vendor

We don't sell and walk away. Every client engagement includes mission planning support, crew training, data processing services, and ongoing technical support. Our field operations team has the domain knowledge to ensure mission success, not just equipment delivery.

In-House Pilots | Training Programs | Dedicated Technical Support
